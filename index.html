Fill it out on your phone ahead of time, with a signal — a PC and a phone don't share this storage, so setting it up on a computer won't carry over to your phone. Once it's loaded here once, it keeps working with zero cell service after that — closed tab, airplane mode, phone restart, doesn't matter. For the smoothest experience, add it to your Home Screen — steps below.
How do I add this to my Home Screen? (and why did my data disappear?)
iPhone — Safari
Tap the Share icon (square with an arrow up, in the bottom toolbar) → scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen → Add.
iPhone — DuckDuckGo, Chrome, or another browser
Tap the ⋮ or ••• menu in the browser's toolbar → Add to Home Screen. Don't see it? Open this same page in Safari once instead and use its Share icon — that always has the option.
Android
Tap the ⋮ menu (top-right of the browser) → Add to Home Screen or Install app.
Using DuckDuckGo? Read this before you fill anything out.
DuckDuckGo can automatically erase a site's saved data — that's what its flame/fire button does, and it can trigger automatically when the app closes. If your entries disappeared after adding this to your Home Screen, that's almost certainly why. Fix it by marking this site Fireproof first: tap the site info/shield icon next to the address bar → Fireproof This Site — then fill out your profile and tags again. Menu wording varies by DuckDuckGo version; look for "Fireproof."
If a menu item above isn't worded exactly the same on your phone, look for anything mentioning "Home Screen," "Install," or "Fireproof."
